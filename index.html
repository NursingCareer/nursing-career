🧮 Drug Calculation Hub

Practice essential nursing medication calculations

💊 Dose Calculation

Formula: (Desired Dose ÷ Available Dose) × Quantity

💧 IV Flow & Drip Rate

mL/hr = Volume ÷ Hours
gtt/min = Volume × Drop Factor ÷ Minutes

⚖️ Weight-Based Dose

Dose = Weight × mg/kg/dose

🔄 Drug Unit Converter

1 g = 1000 mg
1 mg = 1000 mcg

💉 Vial Reconstitution

Concentration = Drug ÷ Volume
Required volume = Dose ÷ Concentration

📐 BSA Dose Calculation

Mosteller BSA = √[(Height × Weight) ÷ 3600]
⚠️ Nursing Safety Notice:
This calculator is intended for nursing education and calculation practice. Always verify medication calculations independently and follow the prescription, medication label, patient-specific assessment, pharmacist review and institutional medication-safety protocols before administering any medication.
