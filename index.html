Viva Preparation

🎤 Nursing Viva

Quick questions for nursing viva and interview preparation.

Q: What is GCS used for?
A: Glasgow Coma Scale is used to assess a patient's level of consciousness.
Q: Why is hand hygiene important?
A: It reduces the transmission of microorganisms and helps prevent healthcare-associated infection.
Q: What should be checked before medication administration?
A: Verify the patient, medication, dose, route, timing and other applicable medication-safety checks according to policy.
Q: What is a vital sign?
A: Common vital signs include temperature, pulse, respiration and blood pressure; oxygen saturation is also routinely monitored in many clinical settings.
