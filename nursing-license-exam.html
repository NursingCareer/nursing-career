High-Yield Notes

Quick revision points for exam preparation.

🫀 Medical-Surgical

Prioritize airway, breathing and circulation. Assess unstable patients first. Monitor vital signs, oxygenation, consciousness and acute changes.

💊 Pharmacology

Before medication administration verify the patient, medication, dose, route, time and documentation. Always check allergies and relevant precautions.

👶 Pediatric

Medication doses in children are commonly weight-based. Confirm weight, concentration and calculated dose before administration.

🤰 Midwifery

Monitor maternal vital signs, fetal status, labor progress and warning signs. Escalate abnormal findings promptly.

🧠 Mental Health

Use therapeutic communication, active listening and a non-judgmental approach. Maintain safety and professional boundaries.

🏥 Community Health

Focus on prevention, health promotion, immunization, sanitation, education and community-level nursing interventions.
