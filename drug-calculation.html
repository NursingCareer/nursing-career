🧮 Important Formulas

Common educational formulas used in nursing calculation practice

Basic Dose Formula

Required amount = (Desired dose ÷ Available dose) × Quantity
IV Flow Rate

Flow rate = Total volume ÷ Time
Infusion Time

Time = Total volume ÷ Flow rate
📌 Practice Example

Suppose an educational question states that a medication order is 500 mg and the available preparation is 250 mg per tablet.

Using the basic formula:

500 ÷ 250 = 2 tablets

⚠️ Important: These examples are for educational practice only. Real medication administration must always follow the prescription, institutional protocol, medication label, and appropriate clinical verification.
