🦴 Skeletal System

Human skeleton-এর structure এবং major bones সম্পর্কে basic ধারণা।

Key Concepts

  • Adult human skeleton contains 206 bones.
  • Skeleton provides support and protection.
  • Bone marrow is important for blood cell formation.
  • Joints allow movement between bones.
  • Cartilage provides support and reduces friction.
  • Axial skeleton includes skull, vertebral column and thoracic cage.
  • Appendicular skeleton includes limbs and their girdles.

💪 Muscular System

Movement, posture এবং বিভিন্ন body function-এর সঙ্গে muscles-এর সম্পর্ক।

Three Major Types of Muscle

  • Skeletal muscle — voluntary movement-এর সঙ্গে প্রধানত যুক্ত।
  • Smooth muscle — internal organs-এর wall-এ পাওয়া যায়।
  • Cardiac muscle — heart-এর muscular wall তৈরি করে।

❤️ Cardiovascular System

Heart এবং blood vessels-এর structure।

Important Anatomy

  • Heart is located in the thoracic cavity between the lungs.
  • Heart has four chambers: right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium and left ventricle.
  • Major blood vessels include aorta, pulmonary arteries, pulmonary veins and venae cavae.
  • Valves help maintain one-way flow of blood.
  • Coronary circulation supplies blood to the cardiac muscle.

🫁 Respiratory System

Main Structures

  • Nose and nasal cavity
  • Pharynx
  • Larynx
  • Trachea
  • Bronchi
  • Bronchioles
  • Lungs
  • Alveoli

Alveoli are the major sites of gas exchange between air and blood.

🧠 Nervous System

Major Divisions

  • Central nervous system — brain and spinal cord.
  • Peripheral nervous system — nerves outside the brain and spinal cord.
  • Autonomic nervous system regulates many involuntary functions.
  • Brain is protected by the skull and meninges.
  • Spinal cord is protected by the vertebral column.

📝 Anatomy MCQ Practice

প্রথম revision-এর জন্য basic Anatomy MCQ।

QUESTION 01
How many bones are present in the adult human skeleton?
A. 201
B. 206
C. 210
D. 216
✓ Answer: B. 206
QUESTION 02
How many chambers are present in the human heart?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
✓ Answer: C. 4
QUESTION 03
The main site of gas exchange in the lungs is the—
A. Trachea
B. Bronchus
C. Alveoli
D. Larynx
✓ Answer: C. Alveoli
QUESTION 04
Which organs form the central nervous system?
A. Brain and spinal cord
B. Brain and muscles
C. Spinal cord and bones
D. Nerves and muscles
✓ Answer: A. Brain and spinal cord
QUESTION 05
Which organ is primarily responsible for urine formation?
A. Liver
B. Kidney
C. Bladder
D. Ureter
✓ Answer: B. Kidney
QUESTION 06
Which gland is commonly called the master gland?
A. Thyroid
B. Adrenal
C. Pituitary
D. Pineal
✓ Answer: C. Pituitary
QUESTION 07
Which organ is the main site of bile production?
A. Stomach
B. Pancreas
C. Liver
D. Gallbladder
✓ Answer: C. Liver
QUESTION 08
Which organ stores bile?
A. Liver
B. Gallbladder
C. Pancreas
D. Stomach
✓ Answer: B. Gallbladder
